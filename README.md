# HabiTree – Sip ’n Grow

**A fun bottle-filling app that helps students build healthy habits while growing virtual trees.**

---

## 🧭 Project Overview
**Theme:** Sustainability at School  
**App Name:** HabiTree – Sip ’n Grow

HabiTree encourages students to refill their water bottles regularly. Each bottle fill gives the student Growth Points and makes a virtual tree grow. The app helps students build healthy drinking habits while learning about protecting the environment.

---

## ✨ Unique Selling Point (USP)
- Every refill = virtual tree growth 🌳  
- Makes sustainability fun and rewarding for kids  
- Easy and colorful interface for school use  
- Optional classroom leaderboard to reach team goals

---

## 🔎 What the App Does
- Track each time a student fills their bottle.  
- Add Growth Points for every refill.  
- Show a virtual tree growing through stages (seed → sprout → small tree → big tree).  
- Display daily/weekly progress and fun sustainability tips.

---

## 📦 Features
- `Sip 'n Grow` button to record a refill  
- Personal virtual garden (your trees)  
- Daily and weekly goals & streaks  
- Simple sustainability tips and facts  
- Optional school leaderboard (class vs class)
